## What sponsoring does and does not buy

The code is MIT and stays MIT. Sponsoring buys my time, never the licence: everything
here is free to use commercially, today and after you stop sponsoring. No feature is
held back for sponsors, and no release is delayed to create one.

What it changes is which issue I open first on a Saturday, and whether your company
name sits in this file and in the README.

I work alone, on modest hardware, so time is the thing in short supply. That is what
you are funding.
